Settle never says a seller or buyer "is protected". It says which of these checks pass and that **PayPal decides eligibility**. Both Seller Protection pages say so: "PayPal determines, in its sole discretion, whether your claim is eligible".

## Seller Protection (ship gate and dispute checklist)

| ID | Requirement | US clause (US-SP) | UK clause (UK-SP) | How Settle checks it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SP-ADDR | Ship to the address on the transaction | Basic Requirements: "You must ship the item to the shipping address on the Transaction Details page in your PayPal account for the transaction. If you originally ship the item to the recipient's shipping address on the Transaction Details page but the item is later redirected to a different address, you will not be eligible" | Basic Requirements: same wording ("transaction details page") | Postcode or ZIP on the PayPal order's shipping address vs any in messages since payment and the one typed from the label. Mismatch → HOLD. |
| SP-BUYER-SHIP | Don't use buyer-arranged shipping | "We therefore recommend not using a shipping service that is arranged by the buyer, so that you will be able to provide valid proof of shipping and delivery." | Same wording | A message asking to use the buyer's courier or a pickup → warning. This is a recommendation, not a requirement, so it's worded as advice. |
| SP-PROOF-SHIP | Proof of shipment | Establishing Proof, Physical Goods: shipping-company documentation with "An online and verifiable tracking number", "Date of shipment", "An address for the recipient that matches the shipping address on the Transaction Details page" **and** "An address for the recipient showing at least the city/state, city/country, or zip/postal code" | Same, but the address items are joined by "**or**" | Tracking number added to the PayPal order (`trackers`), ship date, and the destination postcode matches the PayPal address. |
| SP-PROOF-DELIV | Proof of delivery (required for Item Not Received) | Item Not Received Additional Requirement: "You must provide proof of delivery as described below." Proof of delivery: "An online and verifiable tracking number", "Date of delivery and 'delivered' status", and a matching recipient address | Same | Tracker status shows delivered (from PayPal or the carrier) at the PayPal address. "Not yet" until then. |
| SP-CARRIER | Carrier must show "delivered" | "Please ensure, especially when shipping goods internationally, that your carrier can provide 'delivered' status at the correct address, or your Seller Protection claim may be denied." | Same | Advice shown when the seller picks a carrier or untracked delivery. |
| SP-RESPOND | Respond to PayPal on time | "You must respond to PayPal's requests for documentation and other information in a timely manner … If you do not respond … you may not be eligible" | Same ("Resolution Centre") | Dispute case file shows the `seller_response_due_date` deadline. |
| SP-SHIP-TIME | Ship promptly | "If the sale involves pre-ordered or made-to-order goods, you must ship within the timeframe you specified in the listing. Otherwise, it is recommended that you ship all items within 7 days after receipt of payment." | Same ("seven days") | Days since capture shown; over 7 → advice (a recommendation, not a fail). A shipping date on the deal card → must ship by it. |
| SP-PHYSICAL | Physical, shippable goods | "The item must be a physical, tangible good that can be shipped, except for items subject to the Intangible Goods Additional Requirements." | Same | Delivery method on the card. Digital or service deals → "can't check". |
| SP-INPERSON | In-person handover excluded | Ineligible: "Physical, tangible items delivered in person, including in connection with a payment made in your physical store, unless the buyer paid for the transaction in person using PayPal's goods and services QR code." | Same | Delivery method "collect" or "meet up" → FAIL on the checklist. Settle doesn't use QR codes, so the exception never applies. |
| SP-SNAD | Not-as-described claims are excluded | Ineligible: "Items where the buyer claims (either with us or their card issuer) that the item you sent isn't what was ordered (referred to as a Significantly Not as Described claim)." | Same | M4: for "not as described", the case file says Seller Protection doesn't cover it. The evidence is the confirmed card, its quotes and the order's item description. |
| SP-FF | Friends and Family payments excluded | Ineligible: "Personal payments including a payment sent using PayPal's friends and family functionality." | Same ("friend and family") | Payment must arrive as a captured Orders v2 checkout for this deal. A chat asking for F&F → warning to both sides. |
| SP-SINGLE | One payment, one account | — (not in US-SP) | Basic Requirements: "You must accept a single payment from one PayPal account for the purchase (partial payment and/or payment in instalments are excluded)." | **UK only:** one order per deal, captured once for the full card amount. Deposits or split payments → warning. |
| SP-NOT-PP | Payments outside the buyer's PayPal account | Ineligible: "Payments not processed through a buyer's PayPal account, including PayPal Guest Checkout payments and Standard Credit and Debit Card Payments where the seller's account is registered in the following countries – Singapore, China, … Luxembourg." Other countries: "otherwise eligible" | Same list | US and UK aren't on the list, so guest and card checkout stay eligible. No check needed; listed for completeness. |
| SP-RELEASE | Don't ship after PayPal says not to | Ineligible: "Items sent after PayPal advised you not to release the item." | Same | Ship gate: an open dispute or a capture that isn't `COMPLETED` → HOLD. |
| SP-COUNTRY | Seller's account country | "The primary address for your PayPal account must be in the United States." | "The primary address for your PayPal account must be in one of the countries to which this user agreement applies." | The seller's country is set on the deal. We can't read the account address, so "can't check". |

### Signature thresholds

**None in the current terms.** "Signature" doesn't appear anywhere in the six pages above as of 2026-10-18; it was searched for. Settle has no signature-at-amount check and doesn't invent one. If a later version adds one, add a row here and re-check before submission.

### Excluded items (both programs)

Both US-SP and UK-SP list as ineligible: real estate; vehicles (except "personally portable light vehicles used for recreational purposes like bicycles and wheeled hoverboards"); businesses; industrial machinery; "Payments equivalent to cash, including stored value items such as gift cards and pre-paid cards"; gold; financial products or investments; NFTs (with a $10,000 rule); gambling; donations; payments to state-run bodies; bill payment services; Payouts. US-SP also lists "Payments you receive using the account and routing number assigned to your PayPal business account". Both also list travel tickets in some unauthorized-transaction cases.

Settle check (ID **SP-EXCLUDED**): an item on the card that matches a vehicle, gift card or pre-paid card, gold, real estate or ticket keyword → checklist row "excluded item" FAIL, citing this list. A keyword match is a hint, so it shows the matched word.

## Purchase Protection (US) and Buyer Protection (UK)

| ID | Requirement | US clause (US-PP, US-UA) | UK clause (UK-BP, UK-UA) | How Settle uses it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PP-FF | Friends and Family isn't covered | US-PP Ineligible: "Personal Payments including payments sent using PayPal's friends and family functionality." US-UA (Send Money): "You must not send money as a personal transaction (often referred to as using the 'Send Money to a Friend or Family Member' feature in your PayPal account) when you are paying for goods or services." | UK-BP Ineligible: same wording ("friend and family"). UK-UA: "Where you send money from your PayPal account to another PayPal account as a 'friends and family' payment, you are not entitled to be refunded under either the rules or the Buyer Protection Program." | Buyer warning when the chat asks for F&F or "as a gift". |
| PP-SELLER-FF | Sellers must not ask for F&F | US-UA, Receiving payments: "Not ask your buyer to send you money as a personal transaction (often referred to as using the 'Send Money to a Friend or Family Member' feature)." | UK-UA, Restricted Activities: "Request or send a personal transaction payment for a commercial transaction." | Same signal, quoted to the seller too: asking for F&F breaks PayPal's terms. |
| PP-OFF-PAYPAL | Must pay from the buyer's PayPal account | US-PP Eligibility: "Pay for the eligible item from your PayPal account." Ineligible: "Payments that you have not sent using your PayPal account." | UK-BP: same wording | Off-PayPal payment (bank transfer, Zelle, Cash App, crypto, gift cards, other payment links) → buyer warning. The only payment path is Settle's PayPal checkout. |
| PP-COVERS | What's covered | "Item Not Received" and "Significantly Not as Described" claims | Same | Settle's own buyer-side wording (not a PayPal quote): "Paying through this checkout keeps the payment eligible to be considered under PayPal's program; PayPal decides." |
| PP-COLLECT | Collection in person isn't covered for Item Not Received | US-PP: "your purchase is not eligible for Item Not Received claims if you use PayPal or PayPal World to pay in person, or if you order online, but collect the item in person, or arrange for it to be collected on your behalf." | UK-BP: "Item Not Received claims for physical, tangible items you collect in person or arrange to be collected on your behalf." | Card with "collect" or "meet up" delivery → buyer note: Item Not Received isn't covered for collection. |
| PP-CONTACT | Contact the seller first | "Attempt to contact the seller to resolve your issue directly before filing a claim through the Resolution Center." | Same ("Resolution Centre") | M4 case file: the chat record shows contact before the dispute. |
| PP-TIMEFRAME | When disputes can be opened | Item Not Received: "within 180 days of the date you sent the payment". Not as Described: "(i) within 30 days of the date of delivery or fulfillment … or (ii) within 180 days … whichever is sooner." Escalate "within 20 days after opening the dispute". | Same timeframes | M4 shows these dates on the case file (informational). |
| PP-SNAD-DEF | What counts as Significantly Not as Described | Is: "materially different from the seller's description", "condition of the item was misrepresented", "advertised as authentic but is not authentic", "missing major parts or features and those facts were not disclosed". Isn't: "The defect in the item was correctly described by the seller", "properly described but did not meet your expectations", "minor scratches and was described as 'used.'" | Same lists | M4 "not as described" checklist: card terms with source quotes (condition, authenticity, included parts) vs the item description on the PayPal order. |
| PP-EXCLUDED | Excluded purchases | Similar list to Seller Protection, plus "Significantly Not as Described claims for wholly or partly custom-made items" and "Items intended for resale" | Same | Same keyword check as SP-EXCLUDED, warning the buyer. |
